Website: https://adrian-li-portfolio2026-2028.vercel.app/

# 6. Case Study Storytelling

The portfolio should move from:

> “Here is what I designed.”

to:

> **“Here is the complex problem I solved, how I made decisions, and what changed.”**

Recommended case study framework:

## 1. Context

What product, users and business environment were involved?

## 2. Problem

What was broken or unclear?

## 3. Complexity

What made the problem difficult?

Examples:

- Multiple payment rails
- Compliance requirements
- Risk
- Cross-border differences
- Large data sets
- Multiple user roles
- Business constraints

## 4. Design Challenge

Use a clear question:

> How might we help users make confident financial decisions without increasing cognitive load?

## 5. Design Strategy

Explain the principles and system-level decisions.

## 6. System / UX Architecture

Show:

- Information architecture
- User flows
- Decision hierarchy
- Design system
- Risk signals
- Interaction model

## 7. Outcome

Show measurable business/product impact.

This structure is much stronger for Staff/Lead hiring than a visual gallery.
